OPERATION GOBBLE

How to play

  1. Pick a mission. Open your dossier and choose one of your three secret tasks.
  2. Do it quietly. Keep the goal to yourself. When you finish, tap Mark as completed and confirm to earn points.
  3. Need a different mission? Tap Abandon to give it up for 25 XP. Once the leader starts the game, abandoned and completed missions get replaced while cards are available.
  4. Cover blown? If someone figures out what you’re trying to do, tap Sniffed out. The mission ends and you lose 50 XP.

Field reports are on the honor system. HQ trusts you to log only the missions you actually pulled off.
